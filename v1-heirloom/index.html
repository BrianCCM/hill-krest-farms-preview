The farm, and the people who keep it

Two or three sentences in the owner's own words about why they started hosting, and what they want a day here to feel like.

What has not changed is the ground itself. Cottonwoods along the ditch, fields that turn gold in October, and a sky that does most of the decorating for you.

Three spaces, one afternoon

A ceremony on the lawn, dinner under the tents, and dancing in the barn when the wind comes up. Everything is a short walk from everything else.

The ceremony lawn

Open grass with the tree line behind it, set for up to outdoor capacity guests. Chairs are set facing direction so the afternoon sun is behind the camera.

The barn hall

Open trusses, string lights and a chandelier, seating indoor capacity at tables. Heated, so a November wedding works as well as a June one.

The tented yard

Frame tents on the gravel between the barn and the lawn, for dinner service, a bar or a shaded place to sit. Included or priced separately.

What a rental includes

You are renting the ground, the buildings and what is already standing on them. Everything below comes with the day.
