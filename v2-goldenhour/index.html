Three spaces, one afternoon

The ceremony lawn

Open grass with the tree line behind it, set for up to outdoor capacity guests. Chairs face direction, so the late sun lands behind the camera instead of in your guests' eyes.

The barn hall

Open trusses, string lights and a chandelier, seating indoor capacity at tables. Heated, which is why a November wedding works here as well as a June one.

The tented yard

Frame tents on the gravel between the barn and the lawn, for dinner service, a bar, or somewhere shaded to sit out the hottest part of the afternoon. Included or priced separately.
